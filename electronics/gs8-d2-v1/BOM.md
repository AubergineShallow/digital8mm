**Status:** planning only. Nothing was bought, no cart was used, no supplier was contacted. Currency **SGD**, including a 9 % GST allowance; shipping excluded. Lines priced from prices.json carry its id, date and confidence (see its legend; USD list prices x 1.2799 x 1.09). Every other figure is marked **estimate**: a USD planning figure written in the script, not looked up and not a quote. "unpriced" lines have no figure.

**Done on this machine vs not done (r3).** Done: this generated list, the CAD and its computed checks, the planning calculations (WIRING s4). Not done: procurement, printing or slicing, measurement, soldering, power-up and every bench gate (WIRING s9, MEASURED-PARTS). Candidate lines (quantity 0) are not adopted.
