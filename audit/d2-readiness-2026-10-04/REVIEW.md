## Priority findings and acceptance criteria

Priorities refer to the next development pass. A concern can block a claim of full build readiness without blocking a useful coupon print. Confirmed facts, unverified mechanisms and optional design changes are identified separately.

### 1 Pi service is deliberately destructive

**High priority — confirmed construction limitation.**

The four Pi-stack retention hooks have no release access. The assembly instructions say removing the stack destroys the tub hooks and requires a tub reprint. This is the clearest conflict with reasonable maintenance. It trades a small screw count for an expensive service operation. See [ASSEMBLY.md:243](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/ASSEMBLY.md:243>).

**Improvement avenues:** investigate accessible release windows or relocate the retention points; alternatively use a removable keeper with the same common screwdriver. A small increase in reusable hardware is reasonable if it eliminates destructive removal. Keep the stack seated on positive locating surfaces so the latch does not also carry positioning loads.

**Acceptance:** document and demonstrate stack insertion and removal with the specified tools, without damaging the enclosure, PCB or nearby cables. Repeat the intended service cycles on printed parts. Re-run insertion, clearance and driver-access checks for the revised sequence.

### 2 The thin-wall pass does not establish minimum local thickness

**High priority — confirmed test limitation and documented unresolved geometry.**

The screen permits a small area of under-thickness material. Current reported minima include skirt key necks at **0.68–0.71 mm**, base skirt-groove lips at **0.77 mm**, and a cap detent feature at **0.70 mm**. The skirt/groove and panel-edge issues remain open. These are not all equivalent: a nonstructural detent bump should not be treated as a load-bearing key. See [SPEC.md:153](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/SPEC.md:153>) and [checks.py:302](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/checks.py:302>).

There is a further coverage gap: `loaded_boxes()` only defines loaded zones for the panel, tub and hood. The base/grip and cap receive **zero loaded samples**, and the code maps no loaded samples to a zero under-thickness share. The separate boss check does cover screw-boss details, but it does not establish every grip, strap, keyhole or battery-retention load path. See [checks.py:353](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/checks.py:353>) and [checks.py:368](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/checks.py:368>).

**Improvement avenues:** identify critical load paths feature by feature; add explicit zones and deterministic sections at their narrowest points. Add a missing-coverage failure for features designated load-bearing. Strengthen or reshape the skirt key/groove and resolve the panel end locally. Keep documented, justified exceptions for nonstructural details without using the global share rule to justify critical local weaknesses.

**Acceptance:** report measured local sections and meaningful coverage for each designated critical feature. Print and test the revised retention interfaces. Add skirt/groove and cap-retention test pieces, or explicitly use small whole parts and mating sections: the existing twelve coupon filenames do not include these interfaces.

### 3 Opening the panel depends on an inaccessible skirt barb

**Medium priority — confirmed access dependency, unverified release behavior.**

The left skirt must be pulled rearward before the panel can open. Its barb has no tool-release access; the plan relies on it riding out of its recess under pull force. That force and repeated release behavior have not been demonstrated. See [ASSEMBLY.md:230](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/ASSEMBLY.md:230>).

**Improvement avenues:** make the retaining barb accessible, make the skirt removable without forced flexure, or redesign the base edge to eliminate the skirt dependency. Do not simply delete a skirt: it currently interacts with the joint and panel access.

**Acceptance:** a documented, nondestructive panel-opening operation with specified tool access and repeatable release. Check it with the strap installed. Prefer a service path that does not disturb optics unless those optics are being serviced.

### 4 The stated peak power budget exceeds the stated supply ceiling

**High priority before a powered full-build claim — confirmed document-level mismatch, not an observed electrical failure.**

The wiring document budgets **27 W peak** on the 5.1 V side, while stating that the X1203 is limited to **25.5 W**. Calling the output capped does not show that a 27 W demand is successfully served. See [WIRING.md:72](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/electronics/gs8-d2-v1/WIRING.md:72>).

**Improvement avenues:** define the actual simultaneous recording workload and transient envelope. Either substantiate a lower load limit with operating restrictions, or select a power arrangement with appropriate headroom and recheck its geometry. Do not erase the planning peak solely to make the budget pass.

**Acceptance:** measure the intended camera, storage, EVF and cooling workload together across the intended battery range, including startup and write bursts. Record rail behavior, resets, throttling, connector/boost temperatures and shutdown behavior against the existing bench-gate criteria. The audit does not claim that a brownout has occurred.

### 5 Selected component interfaces are not yet frozen

**High priority before committing to the complete print set — existing physical validation gates.**

Examples include the unspecified battery-pack SKU and envelope, assumed X1203 pad positions and 10.9 mm standoffs, camera mounting-hole uncertainty, HDMI plug dimensions, and run-button cap removal needed by assembly. See [WIRING.md:243](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/electronics/gs8-d2-v1/WIRING.md:243>) and [DESIGN.md:249](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/DESIGN.md:249>).

The EVF is especially consequential: it combines a panel removed from a kit prism, an HDMI board and a separate eyepiece. Panel release, board revision, optical alignment and electrical behavior remain conditional. This is more involved than installing a proven complete EVF cartridge. See [EVF-SELECTION.md:19](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/electronics/gs8-evf-v1/EVF-SELECTION.md:19>) and [EVF-SELECTION.md:88](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/electronics/gs8-evf-v1/EVF-SELECTION.md:88>). The referenced EVF record also explicitly says the diode supply's upper voltage bound is not established on paper; retain that gate rather than accepting only a typical diode drop ([EVF-SELECTION.md:134](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/electronics/gs8-evf-v1/EVF-SELECTION.md:134>)).

**Improvement avenues:** make a concise measured-parts record, with the exact variant, photographs, connector envelope and mounting dimensions. Validate the actual EVF as a bench assembly before freezing its final carrier. Update only geometry affected by measured differences.

**Acceptance:** close each interface-dependent gate with real measurements or a demonstrated assembly. CAD cannot substitute for evidence that a supplied panel can be removed or that a purchased button cap detaches.

### 6 EVF board restraint needs an explicit check

**Medium priority — geometry-derived question, not a proven failure.**

The board slides into grooves open toward +Y. Its nominal edge is at y=28, while the panel inner plane is at y=32.2; the rails terminate near y=28.5. An explicit +Y keeper was not evident in the reviewed definitions. This suggests a retention question, but **does not prove 4.2 mm of unobstructed motion**: other assembled geometry and connectors must be checked. See [layout.py:75](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/layout.py:75>), [layout.py:227](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/layout.py:227>), and [printed_tub.py:277](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/printed_tub.py:277>).

**Improvement avenue:** identify what arrests motion in all directions. If required, add an integral stop or a controlled compliant pad to an existing part, placed against an appropriate PCB region. Avoid using cable tension as retention.

**Acceptance:** show the opposing restraint in a section view and verify it with the real board and cable set, without loading fragile connectors or the OLED flex.

### 7 Assembly simplicity is weaker than the exterior suggests

**Medium priority for the original brief — confirmed complexity; reductions are design choices.**

There are **12 printed pieces**, comprising **seven enclosure pieces** and **five small printed accessories**. The four body screws are additional to the UPS kit's **eight M2.5 screws and four standoffs**. The design defines ten harnesses. Assembly includes soldering, a socket, tweezers, shaft cutting/filing and a release blade; the BOM states **10 solder joints**, plus two if the pack lacks XT30. “One PH1” accurately describes enclosure closure, not the whole build. See [ASSEMBLY.md:17](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/ASSEMBLY.md:17>), [ASSEMBLY.md:35](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/ASSEMBLY.md:35>), [ASSEMBLY.md:55](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/ASSEMBLY.md:55>) and [BOM.md:106](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/electronics/gs8-d2-v1/BOM.md:106>).

The 21 COTS ledger rows are not a count of physical purchased pieces: one row aggregates four screws and other rows represent kits. Likewise, ten harnesses do not mean ten separately purchased cables.

**Highest-value optional reductions:**

- Put 18/24 selection in the existing push-encoder EVF menu. This can remove the dedicated selector, printed knob, nut/washer, associated lead/junction and two solder joints, as well as shaft cutting and the socket operation. Keep recording and exposure directly accessible; show frame rate clearly in the EVF. This changes the accepted D2 control arrangement, so present it as an option.
- Select a verified shorter camera FPC. The current 200 mm cable requires a nine-layer S-fold; the document already identifies a 100–150 mm alternative as a way to remove that fold. Confirm pinout, contact orientation and relaxed bend space before changing the route. See [WIRING.md:128](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/electronics/gs8-d2-v1/WIRING.md:128>) and [WIRING.md:248](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/electronics/gs8-d2-v1/WIRING.md:248>).
- Investigate eliminating the two skirts through a revised base-edge joint, while retaining useful grip and body separability for print orientation and repair.
- Retain the separate grip if it materially improves printing and service. My integral-grip concepts do not prove that joining it to the body is an overall improvement.

Also align the insert-repair policy with the chosen driver rule: it currently permits a hex screw even though the assembly rules exclude L-keys. See [FASTENER-POLICY.md:66](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/FASTENER-POLICY.md:66>).

### 8 Release status needs separate physical evidence

The problem arises only if that flag is read as a finished-camera guarantee. See [build_d2.py:833](<C:/Users/Pre-Installed User/Claude/Projects/8mm/cad/gs8-d2-v1/build_d2.py:833>).

**Improvement avenue:** expose separate states for CAD checks, slicer review, coupon validation, measured component fit and full assembly/operation. Treat unrun tests as unverified. Keep print time and mass labeled estimates; the recorded roughly 15.5 hours is not a slicer result.

**Acceptance:** retain hash-linked evidence and an honest open-gate list. Do not close gates by amending thresholds alone or by replacing physical test results with another proxy calculation.
