## 2. What D2 is

The D2 "Integral, refined" concept (`concepts/nizo-evf-2026-10-03/D2/CONCEPT.md`), developed into CAD with computed
checks on purchased-part proxies. Print and assembly readiness are open: `status_states` slicer_review,
coupon_validation, measured_fit and assembly_operation are all "not run". The design:
- a satin-silver ASA tub (open to the left) and dial panel, a black hood (roof, eyepiece housing, front plate; r5: no turret) and (r5) a black printed lens collar that carries the lens;
- a black base + grip in one print on 2 keyhole tongues, with a sliding battery cap (r2: no skirts);
- 7 x PT 3.0 x 12 PH1 screws in the whole body (4 close the enclosure, 2 hold the removable Pi keeper, r2, 1 locks the
  base slide, r2 fixer), plus (r5) 4 M3 PH1 lens-collar screws in brass heat-set inserts. "One PH1" covers the **screws only**: the whole build also needs a soldering iron, a socket,
  a saw and file, tweezers, 2 hood release pins, a torque screwdriver, the printed collar centring gauge and more
  (ASSEMBLY s1.1, 14 tools since r6);
- Pi 5 + Active Cooler on a Geekworm X1203 (pogo pins) flat on the floor, located by 4 bosses and held by a removable
  printed keeper (r2); GS camera hanging on the lens (r5, J7-R float; r1-r4: on 2 pins); EVF-A at the rear top;
  USB SSD stick out of a rear scoop; 1S2P 18650 pack + XT30 in the grip;
- controls: run button (GPIO26), exposure encoder (I2C 0x37), 18/24 rotary (GPIO13), power plunger on the Pi button.
  I/O = exposure dial (encoder, with its push switch), 18/24 dial, power plunger (on/off), run (record) button and the EVF; **no LED indicators** (user decision 2026-10-05: the plunger is black ASA, no light pipe; the Pi 5 onboard LEDs are switched off in config.txt; power-on and shutdown are shown on the EVF, WIRING s8).
Body 154 x 70 x 100 mm. Single source of truth: `layout.py`; contract and interfaces: `SPEC.md`.

## 6f. r5 heavy-lens support, 2026-10-06 (`R5-BRIEF.md`; study `research/r5-lens-support/`; log `IMPL-NOTES.md`)

User request: "Fix the sagging issue for the heavy lens first". The study found that the GS lens mount is milled
aluminium; the compliant link is the housing-to-PCB joint (2 small M2 screws, nylon washers, a gasket), and D2's r4
camera model did not match the official drawing (C flange 10 mm too far forward, no front lands, pins that located
nothing). Decision: the **J7-R float** (judge 3's spec, judge 1's details).
- **J7 contract.** The printed `lens_collar` holds the lens on its fixed rear band and sets its axial position with a
  45 deg cone; it is anchored on the tub front wall (TL, TR, LL bolted by s_c1..s_c3 into M3 heat-set inserts; LR a
  compression foot; the axis 16.0 inside the anchor polygon). The camera body, BFAR and adapter touch no printed part
  at any BFAR screw-out s 0..3.0; the tub lip (0.5 ahead of the BFAR face), the hood roll fin (0.8 to the cover) and
  the panel keeper (0.5 behind the cover at s 3.0) are catches. Any creep or flex in the printed parts moves lens and
  sensor together: an aim change, never a focus tilt.
- **Parts.** tub: lip + counterbore teardrops, 3 insert bosses (LL moved from (24.5, 36) to (28, 36) to clear the Pi 5
  box by 0.3), no pins. hood: no turret, 4 foot holes, roll fin + 2 webs. panel: keeper moved. New `lens_collar`
  (`printed_collar.py`, one per lens with a `support` band). `CAM` and the `gs_camera` proxy rebuilt as tagged
  sub-solids parametric in s; the tripod block comes off at bench step B0. `LENSES` corrected (Kowa focus ring at the
  rear, iris at the front, each with an M2 thumb screw; knurl dia 42 at +2.2..+7.6 is the fixed band); Computar
  H6Z0812 as a data-only entry.
- **Fasteners.** 4 M3 screws (ISO 7045, PH1) + washers into brass inserts (FASTENER-POLICY I); every `SCREWS` row has a
  `kind`; PT counts and PT checks apply to the PT kind only.
- **Checks.** `j7_float`, `lens_support`, `lens_clamp`, `inserts` (SPEC s6 5h), required by the J7 joint; the J7 ring
  zones, pins and turret rows were replaced by their real-camera equivalents (RECTIFICATION r5); `boss_geometry` is

How to rebuild (repo root; every CadQuery process through `run_locked.py`, foreground):
1. `python cad/gs8-d2-v1/layout.py` (plain-python self-check, 129 checks, < 1 s).
2. `.venv-cad/Scripts/python.exe cad/gs8-d2-v1/run_locked.py -- cad/gs8-d2-v1/build_d2.py` (full: checks, STL, STEP,
   manifests, receipt, renders; about 250 s since the engraving grow and the new checks, peak about 1 GB). Options:
   `--fast` (no STEP or renders, about 210 s),
   `--lens fujinon_hf6xa`, `--sweep-step 1.0`, `--part <id> --fast` (one part).
3. `.venv-cad/Scripts/python.exe cad/gs8-d2-v1/run_locked.py -- cad/gs8-d2-v1/make_coupons.py` (coupons, about 4 min).
4. `python cad/gs8-d2-v1/make_tables.py` (then `--check` exits 1 if a generated block is stale) and
   `python electronics/gs8-d2-v1/make_bom.py`.

Commands (repo root): `python cad/gs8-d2-v1/layout.py`; `.venv-cad/Scripts/python.exe cad/gs8-d2-v1/run_locked.py --
cad/gs8-d2-v1/build_d2.py`; `python cad/gs8-d2-v1/make_tables.py`; `python electronics/gs8-d2-v1/make_bom.py`.
