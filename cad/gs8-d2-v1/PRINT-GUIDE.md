# GS8 D2 release v1: print guide (`cad/gs8-d2-v1`)

Release r5, 2026-10-06 (first written 2026-10-04 by the docs owner; r4 adds the modifier meshes of s1.1; r5 adds the
lens collar, the tub insert bosses and the hood without its turret). How to print the 12 printed production parts of D2 (count: DESIGN.md s1.1 `counts`). `build_d2.py` writes one STL per part with
the print orientation built in (`out/stl/<part>.stl`: Z0 is the bed and the "face down" of the table is on the bed);
load them unrotated. The tables between `BEGIN`/`END` markers are generated by `make_tables.py` from `layout.py` and
the build's `out/print-manifest.json`. While a module is still a stub, its row shows the **layout estimate** (shell area x
wall x feature factor) instead of the solid. Re-run `python cad/gs8-d2-v1/make_tables.py` after every build.

Nothing here has been printed. Times and filament are estimates; re-slice to get real figures.

## Cloud-polish print stock and finish datums

This document in the copied `r5-cloud-polish-20261007` fork does not supersede any later desktop work. For the lens
collar only, the production STL and `collar_part` coupon include three 0.2 mm sacrificial membranes across the
3.4 mm anchor holes, inside the first washer-seat bridge layer. This gives bridge lines a continuous roof instead
of ending in a hole. Clear only those three holes to 3.4 mm by hand and remove debris before screws or inserts are
assembled. Leave the washer seat planes, cone and feet unchanged. STEP/assembly checks describe the cleared part;
`print-manifest.json` records print stock, finished volume and postprocessing separately. Slicer and coupon gates
remain open: inspect one complete bridge layer and a flat washer seat in the actual slicer and G-COL-1.

The collar rear-bore land is 1.6 mm before the 45-degree seat cone, removing the unused thin entry feather.
Do not enlarge or chamfer it by hand; it is part of the checked profile. The lens seat and band datum have not moved.

The s_c4 upper-lug bearing edge deliberately stays square so its washer is fully supported. Use normal elephant-foot
compensation there and remove only loose burrs, never a chamfer beneath the washer. All other bed-edge rules remain.
The existing envelope, part count, washer type, front cone, fixed-band datum and PH1 driver access are preserved.

The panel encoder cradle now has 1.0 mm tooth lands (1.62 mm probed root), preserving the catch plane while extending
the free tips 0.6 mm. Use the regenerated encoder-cradle coupon and repeat G-SNAP-2; old coupons do not validate it.
The included DejaVu Sans Bold font file is hash-pinned, so the printed lettering is reproducible across computers.

### 1.1 Modifier meshes (r4)

FASTENER-POLICY sets 4 perimeters and a 40 % infill modifier 8 mm round every PT pilot. The build now exports those
zones as meshes in each part's print pose (`layout.print_modifiers`, the `print_modifiers` check confirms each one
overlaps its part), so nobody draws them by hand:

| Part | Modifier files (`out/stl/modifiers/`) | Zones |
|---|---|---|
| `tub` | `tub__mod_s_b1`, `_s_b2`, `_s_r1`, `_s_r2`, `_s_k1`, `_s_k2`, `_s_j`, (r5) `_s_c1`, `_s_c2`, `_s_c3` | dia 16 cylinders along every screw that passes the tub: the s_r counterbore pads, the 2 keeper bosses, the j4 lock boss, the floor round s_b1/s_b2 and (r5) the 3 lens-collar insert bosses in the front wall |
| `panel` | `panel__mod_s_b1`, `_s_b2`, `_s_r1`, `_s_r2` | the 4 threaded bosses and posts (the policy applied to the panel for the first time in r4; before, the panel was printed at plain 25 %) |
| `base_grip` | `base_grip__mod_s_b1`, `_s_b2`, `_s_j`, `_tripod_nut` | the 3 counterbores and the tripod-nut pocket (8 mm round) |

Import the part STL, then add each modifier as a modifier/part-modifier mesh **without moving it** (same bed frame;
if the slicer auto-centres imports, turn that off or move part and modifiers together). Set the modifier to 40 %
gyroid infill and 4 perimeters. The other 9 parts print at 100 % (or as a declared thin part) and have none (r5: the
lens collar is a small part at 100 %; its lug insert sits in solid material). A
slicer-review record names the part STL and all its modifier files (evidence/README.md).

## 4. Hardware fitted to printed parts

- **Tripod nut** (1/4-20 hex, steel): pressed into the base pocket from the top at assembly step 3 with a flat bar. No
  heat. The 2.3 mm bearing wall under it is part of the base.
- **PT screws** need no preparation: the seven 2.5 mm pilots are printed (4 enclosure + 2 keeper + the s_j base lock; r4: was "six", stale since r2). Do not drill or tap them. The first drive
  forms the thread (FASTENER-POLICY A-C).
- **Heat-set inserts (r5, lens collar only; FASTENER-POLICY I).** 3 short M3 inserts (L 4.0, BOM D2-35) go into the
  3 tub front-wall bosses (TL, TR, LL) from the front, and 1 M3 insert L 5.7 (D2-36) into the collar's lower lug
  from below, with the iron's M3 heat-set tip (D2-74), square to the face, 0.1 below it (ASSEMBLY B1). The printed
  bores are 4.0; do not drill them. Everywhere else the M3 insert is only the fallback for a stripped PT boss
  (FASTENER-POLICY E).

## 5. Post-processing checklist

1. Remove the brims and the hood supports; deburr the seam chamfers. Leave the datum faces untouched: the tub
   front-wall outer face (r5: the lens-collar seat, x -2.7; r4 seated the camera on the inner face x -5.2), the tub
   rear outer face (EVF flange datum F) and the wall tops (hood seat).
2. Check the bores with a gauge or the part itself: r5 camera lip dia 32.4 and counterbore dia 37.5 in the tub front
   wall and the hood plate bore dia 36.5 (the camera must not touch them once it hangs on the lens: lip 0.5 ahead of
   the BFAR face, 0.75-0.825 radial); the 4 hood foot holes dia 8.6 (the collar feet pass with 0.5 radial); EVF bore dia 29.3 (spigot locates, 0.15); plunger hole 11.6 x 6.6
   (the stem slides, 0.3). Ream lightly only if a part binds.
3. Scrape or file the hood J2 groove floor (the bridged lip top) flat and check the panel tongue slides in it (0.4
   under the tongue, 0.25 over it). Seat a PT screw head on each counterbore shoulder (tub s_r1/s_r2 from the right,
   base s_b1/s_b2 from below): it must sit flat, with no sag on the bridged shoulder. Check the sliding joints dry
   before any electronics go in: hood rail groove on the panel tongue; base
   keyhole windows on the tub tongues, then the 10 mm slide (0.25); cap rails and
   detent (0.25); stick sleeve in the guide.
4. Check the snap hooks once by hand (hood hooks hk1-hk4 on the tub ledges): they must click with the hood level. (r2: the Pi floor hooks and gate G-SNAP-1
   are withdrawn; the Pi keeper is screwed, nothing on it flexes.) Strains: `layout.snap_strains()`.
   The hook catch faces are short horizontal overhangs in print (hood 0.65 x 8 with a 1.0 land since the r2 fixer; hk3/hk4
   catch faces are 45 deg; encoder cradle 0.65): clean any droop with a craft knife. Check the 2 dia 1.6 release holes in
   the tub right wall take a 1.5 pin with light friction (the pin must stay in by itself).
5. Paint-fill the panel engraving (assembly step 2): paint pen, then wipe the face with IPA on a flat pad.
   The engraving (from `layout.ENGRAVE`, 0.4 deep in the panel face):

### 6.1 Calibration and fit records (r3, audit 2026-10-05 correction 1B)

Three coupon groups (`knob_bore_ladder_enc` + `knob_bore_ladder_sw`, `clearance_comb`, `tongue` + `keyhole_slot`) had no record id before r3; each group now has its own id (the ids `build_d2.py` `CALIBRATION_ITEMS` requires as
`coupon_validation` items), and its result is filed like every other coupon gate:

| Record id | Coupon STLs | What is recorded | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G-KNOB-1** | `knob_bore_ladder_enc`, `knob_bore_ladder_sw` | which rim-notch step (1-5) pushes onto the real encoder and the real 18/24 shaft without play, per shaft | one step fits both shafts (or one per shaft); the chosen offset goes into `FDM['KNOB_BORE_OFFSET']`, then the knobs are rebuilt and their new STL hashes recorded |
| **G-COMB-1** | `clearance_comb` | which pegs enter / slide / bind at 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.30 per side | 0.15 locates, 0.25 slides, 0.30 is free (LOCATE / SLIDE / SEAM of `layout.FDM`) on this printer, material and profile |
| **G-J4-1** | `tongue`, `keyhole_slot` | the J4 tongue drops into the keyhole window, slides 10 mm and does not rock or lift | drop in, full 10 mm slide, no rock |

How to file one: write one structured evidence record per tested item as `evidence/README.md` describes (written by
the checks role; use its template): `item` = the record id above (never a file name), `state` = `coupon_validation`,
`artifacts` = {path: sha256} of every coupon STL of that record actually printed (hash the file you sliced), `profile` = printer,
material, slicer profile or variant, `verdict` = pass or fail, `date`, `by`, `notes`. A record counts only for its
item id and the hashes it lists: a record for an older STL (different hash) is stale, and a failed record stays
visible until a newer passing record for the current hashes is filed. Nothing has been printed or recorded on this
machine; all three are **not run**.
