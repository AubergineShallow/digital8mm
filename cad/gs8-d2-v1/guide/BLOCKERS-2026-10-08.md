# D2 assembly blocker review (2026-10-08)

Status: final (2026-10-08, review 16:33-17:00 MPST). Blocker-review agent for the IKEA-guide session; the table ids BX-1..BX-17 and C-1..C-16 are stable.

Scope: physical assemblability of cad/gs8-d2-v1 following ASSEMBLY.md (s2 B0/B1, steps 1-10, s7 service order) and
electronics/gs8-d2-v1/WIRING.md s7. Read-only review; no CAD rebuild. Geometry from `layout.py` (imported), the r6
`out/checks.json` (16:05 build) and the per-part STEP files in `out/step/parts/` (assembly pose). Scripts: session

## Verdict

**1 BLOCKER, 4 MAJOR, 12 MINOR, 16 CLEARED.** The D2 can be assembled, but not exactly as written. The one blocker is step 6: the HDMI plug cannot be mated outside the body and then slid in with the EVF pair. The mated plug hits the tub's EVF bottom rail about 24.5 mm before home. The `evf_pair_in` sweep missed this because the plug is not in its moving set (BX-1). The same clash blocks service item 7 in reverse. The four MAJOR items each need a sequence or hardware change in the guide. (BX-2) The 150 mm QT lead is too short to plug the encoder with the panel held "beside the body". (BX-3) Mating the XT30 at the grip mouth is not length-checked, and the step-1 tie "to a standoff" can leave the junction about 28 mm inside the bay. (BX-4) The step-8 hold "by the metal lens mount" has no clean grip: the 10 mm housing band sits between the front wall and a PCB/cover that stand proud of it, with about 6 mm free below it. (BX-5) Pressing knob_exp on at step 9 loads only the encoder's 0.4 mm cradle hooks, with nothing behind them. The straight-driver rule holds: all 11 screws (including s_c1..s_c4, s_j, s_k1 and s_k2) are computed at their own step in the tool-13 envelope (C-1). No solder joint is needed after the body is closed. Everything here is computed or estimated from CAD proxies: nothing was printed or bought, and no hand ever entered the model.

## Notes

- **Method.** Clashes: a cadquery boolean of a swept box against the assembly-pose part STEP files (`out/step/parts`,
  16:05 build). Lead lengths: a polyline through the centres of the overlaps of consecutive keep-out boxes (the same
  method as `checks.check_cable_routes`), plus explicit end points. Hand access: clearances read from `layout.py`
  and the `COTS` boxes, against a fingertip of 14-16 mm and a thumb of 18-20 mm.
- **BX-1 is the only item that a printed tub would turn into a hard stop.** The cheapest unblock is a sequence
  change. Mate the OLED flex outside, slide the pair in, then plug the HDMI from below through the rail gap (C-6),
  then mate the 5 V PH. Service reverses this: unplug the HDMI through the gap first, then slide the pair out.
- **Suggested guide wording.**
  - Step 6: "Mate the OLED flex outside the body and close the latch. Slide the OLED and the board in together. Then
    push the HDMI plug up into the board through the gap under it. Then mate the 5 V junction and tuck it."
  - Step 8: "Tilt the panel out from its bottom edge. Plug the QT with tweezers."
  - Move from step 9 to step 2: "Press the exposure knob on while your thumb backs the encoder."
  - Step 10: "If the XT30 does not reach out of the grip mouth, stop. Do not mate it blind inside the bay."
- **Not checked** (no evidence in the repo, or too costly for the time box):
  - the OLED kit-flex path during the pair slide;
  - the real Pi 5 microSD slot type (push-push or friction);
  - the X1203 pad position (Q2);
  - real COTS dimensions (every envelope is a proxy until MEASURED-PARTS is filled);
  - the encoder cradle-hook pull-out force (G-ENC-1) and the knob press force (D-bore ladder coupon).
- **Prior audit items.** B-7 (lens hold) and M-V-MPS-3 (board-slot gap) were closed in text, or at the final pose
  only. BX-4 and BX-1 are new physical evidence against those closures, not re-reports.
