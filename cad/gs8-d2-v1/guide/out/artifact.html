0.1Read first

Rules for the whole build (ASSEMBLY s1)

  • Power last: The battery pack goes in only at step 10, after the body is closed. Every lead in steps 1-9 is made with the pack out of the camera. Never plug or unplug an internal lead with the pack connected.
  • Straight driver only: Every screw is driven with a straight PH1 driver along its own axis. No L-keys, hex keys, angled or ball-end drivers. One bench exception: the camera tripod block screws at B0, if they turn out to be hex.
  • Torque: PT screws (plastic, thread-forming): 0.35-0.5 N m, stop at head contact; no threadlocker; do not re-torque. Lens collar M3: s_c1, s_c2, s_c3 0.15 N m, s_c4 0.2 N m, always on the torque screwdriver (tool 13).
  • Lens rule: Never thread the lens into an unsupported camera. Nobody holds the camera: one fingertip pushes the cover centre forward (push only), and the hood tab catch holds the camera through its metal lock tab while the lens turns. Never grip, pinch or turn the cover or the circuit board. Check both paint marks at every lens change.
  • ESD: Grounded mat and wrist strap for steps 1, 4, 6, 7 and bench step B0 (bare boards, OLED flex, camera).
  • Re-assembly: At most 5 drives per plastic boss. Tally every drive on the build sheet; after 5, use the M3 insert fallback (FASTENER-POLICY D-E).
  • Not yet built: Nobody has assembled a D2. These steps are checked by computed insertion sweeps and the straight-driver audit only. Stop and report if a part does not go where the picture shows.

B0Camera and lens on the bench

Bench, before any body part is printed (MP-CAM, G-CAM-1, G-LENS)

Views: A: from the front-left, above; B: from above; C: result, close-up, from the front-left, above

You need: Raspberry Pi Global Shutter camera; C-CS adapter ring 5 mm; C-mount lens (Kowa LM6HC); (11) Paint pen (engraving fill, adapter mark); (12) ESD mat + wrist strap; (5) Multimeter (continuity, diode test, no-short checks)

  1. Measure and photograph the camera and lens as MEASURED-PARTS MP-CAM asks. Record the C-flange-to-cover-rear distance at s = 0 and at the final s.
  2. Keep the tripod block on and clamp the camera to a bench tripod. Support the lens barrel in a padded V-block. Never let the lens hang on the camera alone.
  3. Seat the C-CS adapter firmly in the back-focus ring (BFAR). Draw one paint-pen line across adapter and BFAR.
  4. With the official Pi supply and an external monitor, set back focus with the lens at infinity (target more than 50 m away). Lock the BFAR split-tab screw. Record s; it must lie between 0 and 3.0.
  5. Remove the lens while the camera stays supported. Only then remove the tripod block and bag its 2 screws.

Caution: Plug the camera FPC only with the power off.

Caution: Never screw the BFAR out past s = 3.0. If infinity needs more, stop: record both stops and update CAM before printing.

Caution: If the tripod block will not come off cleanly, stop: the camera model must be updated first.

Check: s recorded, inside 0-3.0; paint mark across adapter and BFAR; tripod block off, 2 screws bagged

Tip BX-12 (buying tool 13): handle dia 30 or less; with a 1/4 in hex holder use a PH1 bit of 50 mm or more whose turned shank (6.5 or less) covers at least the first 8 mm.

B1Heat-set inserts

Bench, tub empty, any time before step 3 (collar insert: before step 7)

Views: A: from the front-left, above; B: from the left; C: result, close-up, from the front-left, above

You need: tub (printed); lens collar (printed); Bag D: 3 x M3 brass heat-set insert, short; Bag E: 1 x M3 brass heat-set insert, L 5.7; (2) Temperature-controlled soldering iron + solder, flux, wick; M3 heat-set tip

  1. Heat the iron with the M3 heat-set tip.
  2. Press 3 short inserts (bag D) into the front-wall bosses TL, TR and LL from the front, square to the face, finishing 0.1 mm below it.

1aFuse the battery pigtail

Step 1, bench wiring (WIRING s7)

You need: XT30 pigtail, 18 AWG silicone, cut to L_cut (XT30U female); fuse 15 A Littelfuse 0251015.MXL; adhesive heat-shrink 3:1; (2) Temperature-controlled soldering iron + solder, flux, wick; M3 heat-set tip; (3) Wire stripper + flush cutter; (4) Heat gun (adhesive heat-shrink); (5) Multimeter (continuity, diode test, no-short checks)

  1. Cut the pigtail to L_cut = 200 mm + the G-W2 pads-to-W length, rounded up to 5 mm (200 mm if the pads are at W). L_cut runs from the pad joint to the XT30 rear and includes the fuse splice.
  2. If the pads-to-W length is more than 25 mm, stop: do not cut. The battery-lead drop would pass 0.15 V (gate G-W5); route the pair under the board straight to the hole, move W, or use 16 AWG, and re-check.
  3. Strip the red conductor about 20-25 mm from the XT30 female.
  4. Trim each fuse lead to 6.5 mm. Lap 5 mm of lead on the stripped conductor; leave a 1.5 mm stub at the fuse body.
  5. Solder each lead (350 C, 5 s at most).
  6. Shrink one adhesive 3:1 sleeve (about 28 mm) over the whole splice.
  7. Meter the red path end to end: less than 0.1 ohm.

Caution: Never re-solder the XT30 female once the fuse is fitted.

Check: red path < 0.1 ohm; splice fully covered

1bPigtail to the X1203 battery pads

Step 1, bench wiring

You need: Geekworm X1203; fused pigtail (1a); Kapton tape, 3 strips; neutral-cure silicone RTV (not acetoxy); (2) Temperature-controlled soldering iron + solder, flux, wick; M3 heat-set tip; (3) Wire stripper + flush cutter; (4) Heat gun (adhesive heat-shrink); (5) Multimeter (continuity, diode test, no-short checks); (12) ESD mat + wrist strap

  1. Solder red to the + battery pad and black to the - pad.
  2. Shrink adhesive heat-shrink over both joints.
  3. Mark W with a pen on the port edge, 31.5 mm from the button-end edge.
  4. Lead the pair on the top face to W, straight in from inside the board (y < 23), not along the port edge: the keeper fingers kf3 and kf4 sit on that edge.
  5. Put one Kapton strip over the board edge at W. Fold the pair round the edge. Tape the leg flat under the board with 2 Kapton strips, straight in from W for about 32 mm, to above the pigtail hole. Keep the pair within 1 mm of the mark. The rest hangs free.
  6. Pads on the underside: run the pair straight under the board to that point and tape it, with no U.
  7. Strain relief: a neutral-cure silicone RTV bead over both joints and the first 8-10 mm of the pair, bonded to the board. Keep it off the pogo pads and the connectors.
  8. Meter + to - on the pigtail: no short.

Caution: The pack is never connected at the bench.

Caution: No tie to a standoff: the RTV bead is the strain relief. Never acetoxy RTV (it corrodes).

Check: no short + to -; joints insulated; U at the W mark (within 1 mm), leg taped flat; RTV over both joints and 8-10 mm of the pair

1fEncoder address, QT lead splice

Step 1, bench wiring

You need: Adafruit 5880 I2C QT rotary encoder; QT lead parts: Adafruit 4397 (sockets) + Pololu 5521 (JST-SH), 4 thin sleeves, 1 adhesive sleeve; (2) Temperature-controlled soldering iron + solder, flux, wick; M3 heat-set tip; (3) Wire stripper + flush cutter; (4) Heat gun (adhesive heat-shrink); (5) Multimeter (continuity, diode test, no-short checks); (12) ESD mat + wrist strap
